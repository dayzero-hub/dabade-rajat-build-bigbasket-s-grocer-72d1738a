Masala Chai

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp black tea leaves
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 2 green cardamom pods

Method

  1. Boil the water in a pan.
  2. Add the tea leaves and let it simmer for two minutes.
  3. Make the tempering:
    1. Lightly crush the cardamom pods.
    2. Crush the ginger to release its juice.
    3. Add both to the simmering water.
  4. Pour in the milk and sugar and bring to a rolling boil.
  5. Strain into cups and serve hot.
